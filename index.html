Who I Am

About Me

KIS
Kothari International School, Noida
B-279, Sector 50 · CBSE + Cambridge + IB

I'm Ahaan Jaiswal, a 12-year-old student at Kothari International School, Noida. I'm in Class 7, and my goal is to become a professional basketball player.

Basketball is not a hobby — it is my career. I train seriously at the I AM Dribbling Drill Academy and compete across school intramurals, inter-school tournaments, academy leagues, and society-level competitions. I have won gold medals at KIS intramurals, the 3x3 PT Sports Academy tournament, and the I AM Mahagun Mirabella 2026 tournament, and placed 2nd at the 4th Ramagya Inter-School U-13 Championship.

Off the court, I compete nationally and internationally in mathematics and science. I reached the Grand Finale of the Mental Math World Cup (World Rank 96, 57 countries), scored in the 99.6th percentile in Maths on the ASSET test, and was a Super Performer at NELTAS M-CAT International Maths Competition. I was also awarded Best Delegate at KMUN 2025.

I believe being well-rounded — analytically sharp, physically disciplined, and articulate — makes you a better athlete and a better person.
